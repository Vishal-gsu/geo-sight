📖 Layer Guide
🌿 NDVI 2019 / NDVI 2024 — Vegetation Health
NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red). Satellites can see Near-Infrared light invisible to the human eye. Healthy plants reflect NIR strongly → high NDVI.
Compare these two layers to see whether Bengaluru's green cover increased or decreased.
Red = urban/bare   Green = dense vegetation
🔴 ΔNDVI Change (Red = Loss)
This layer directly shows WHERE vegetation changed between 2019 and 2024.
Red pixels = areas where NDVI dropped significantly = vegetation was lost (deforestation, urban construction)
White/neutral = no significant change
Green pixels = new vegetation grew (afforestation, seasonal)
Key finding: Mean ΔNDVI = -0.0091 across Bengaluru metro
🏙️ ΔNDBI Urban Growth
NDBI = (SWIR − NIR) / (SWIR + NIR). Built-up areas (concrete, metal roofs, asphalt) reflect Shortwave Infrared (SWIR) strongly.
Orange/Red = areas that became more built-up between 2019 and 2024
White = no urban change
Use this layer to spot which suburbs of Bengaluru grew fastest.
💧 NDWI 2024 — Water Bodies
NDWI = (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR). Water absorbs NIR and reflects Green.
Blue = lakes, rivers, reservoirs. You can spot Ulsoor Lake, Bellandur Lake, and other Bengaluru waterbodies here.
